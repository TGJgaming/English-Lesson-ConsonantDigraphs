What is a Digraph?

🤝

A digraph is when TWO consonant letters stand together to make ONE new sound!

S + H = SH

Let's meet our new letter friends!

The 'SH' Sound

Put your finger on your lips and say "Shhh!"

👟 🚢 🐟

shoes
ship
fish

The 'CH' Sound

Like a moving train! "Choo-choo!"

🪑 🧀 🍫

chair (Silya)
cheese (Keso)
chocolate

The 'TH' Sound

Put your tongue between your teeth!

3️⃣ 👍 🦷

The 'PH' and 'WH' Sounds

PH sounds like "F". WH sounds like "W".
